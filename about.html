My Story

My Journey So Far

My journey into technology hasn't followed a straight path. I originally studied Civil Engineering at Kaduna Polytechnic, but my growing interest in digital products led me to explore UI/UX design.

What started as an interest in how websites and applications look gradually developed into a deeper curiosity about how they work, how people interact with them, and how thoughtful design can solve everyday problems.

Over time, I've had the opportunity to work on real-world digital products, collaborate with teams, and mentor aspiring UI/UX designers.

Today, I work across product design while studying Software Engineering to strengthen my understanding of programming and modern web technologies, including Python, PHP, JavaScript, React, and Next.js.

I'm continuously learning, experimenting, and improving. My long-term goal is to become a versatile product professional who can contribute meaningfully to both the design and development of digital solutions.
